Profile

Registered Architect (M.A.) and member of the Bayerische Architektenkammer with approximately seven years of professional experience in design, permit and execution planning for residential, commercial and mixed-use building projects.

Independent delivery of HOAI service phases 1–5: needs analysis and basic evaluation with the client, development and presentation of the architectural design, permit documentation, and execution and detail planning.

Consistently BIM-based planning with Autodesk Revit across all project phases, including model coordination with structural and MEP engineering as well as liaison with authorities and contractors.

Structured, quality- and results-oriented working style with strong client focus. Based in the Regensburg area.
